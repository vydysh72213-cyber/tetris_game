# Tetris

## History

Tetris was created in 1984 by Soviet programmer Alexey Pajitnov. The game was inspired by Pentominoes, a puzzle involving shapes made from five squares. Pajitnov simplified the idea by using shapes made from four squares, known as tetrominoes. The name “Tetris” combines “tetra,” meaning “four,” with the name of the creator’s favorite sport, tennis.

The game first appeared on the Elektronika 60 computer. It was later adapted for other computers, and in 1989 Tetris became especially well known through the Game Boy handheld console. Its simple rules and engaging gameplay helped it become popular around the world.

Since then, Tetris has appeared on many platforms and in numerous versions. Its central idea has remained the same: arrange falling shapes and complete horizontal lines to clear them.
